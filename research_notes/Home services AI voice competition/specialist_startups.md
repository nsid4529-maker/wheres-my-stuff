# Vertical-specialist AI voice / AI CSR startups for US home services (as of Nov 2026)

Method note: Research was done Nov 23, 2026 using web search only. Direct page fetches to several third-party review sites failed because of network restrictions in the session: idlen.io, contractortoolstack.com and thesnowmedia.com. Many facts below therefore rest on search-result summaries of the cited pages. Treat figures marked "third-party" as unverified. Most pricing and traction figures are either vendor-reported or estimates from comparison blogs, and many of those blogs belong to competing vendors (for example Macha/getmacha, Handler, Carly, NextPhone and ServiceAgent).

## Who are the players, and how does each position, price, integrate, fund and show traction?

### Takeaway
By late 2026 the specialist category has split into well-funded tiers:
- **Enterprise / PE-backed multi-location "AI front office":** Avoca ($1B valuation, Apr 2026), Netic ($450M valuation, Nov 2025) and Probook (dispatch-first, $40M from a16z/Sequoia, Jun 2026).
- **Broad SMB-to-midmarket AI agents:** Broccoli ($52M from Khosla, reported ~$300M valuation, 1,500+ customers).
- **Lead-response / CSR platform acquired by a marketplace:** Hatch, bought by Yelp for $270M cash (closed Feb 2026).
- **Low-priced SMB tools:** Sameday (from $449/mo) and LeadTruffle (from ~$229/mo, text-first).
- **Adjacent in-person sales coaching:** Rilla and Siro. These are not voice agents.

Almost no specialist publishes prices except Sameday and, per third parties, LeadTruffle.

### Inferences
- Two mid-2026 events show where capital and exit value are going:
  - The $1B Avoca round and the Hatch–Yelp exit (~11x ARR on $25M ARR).

- Product scope is converging into a full "AI front office": inbound plus outbound, membership renewal, estimate follow-up and dispatch. The differentiators are depth of ServiceTitan write-back and dispatch-board awareness.

## What do customers complain about, and what are each player's weaknesses?

### Takeaway
Independent review evidence is very thin. Avoca, Sameday and LeadTruffle have essentially no G2 or Capterra reviews. The recurring criticisms come from competitor-authored comparison pages and second-hand Reddit quotes:
- Opaque, usage-based pricing
- Long enterprise onboarding
- Poor fit for small shops
- Text-first rather than voice for some tools
- Dependence on clean FSM data
- "Glorified voicemail" agents that don't write into the CRM

### Cited Findings
- **Avoca:**
  - Onboarding reportedly takes 4–12 weeks, with no free trial — [FieldCamp](https://fieldcamp.ai/reviews/avoca-ai/); [Macha](https://www.getmacha.com/blog/avoca-ai-complete-guide)
  - Usage billing means peak months cost the most — [Macha](https://www.getmacha.com/blog/avoca-ai-complete-guide)
  - Reviewers say to skip it if you have fewer than 3 CSRs, and that it is over-engineered for shops under ~$3M revenue — [gomecha](https://www.gomecha.ai/compare/avoca-ai); [hvactoolsai](https://www.hvactoolsai.com/tools/avoca)
  - No meaningful G2, Capterra or Trustpilot presence as of mid-2026 — [claudessa](https://claudessa.com/vs/avoca)
- **Sameday:**
  - No G2, Capterra or Trustpilot reviews. ToolChase gives an editorial 3.7/5 — [Carly](https://www.usecarly.com/blog/sameday-ai/); [ToolChase](https://toolchase.com/tool/sameday/)
  - The overage rate is undisclosed — [golmtech](https://golmtech.solutions/blog/sameday-ai-answering-service-review/)
- **LeadTruffle:**
  - G2 says there are "not enough reviews" — [G2](https://www.g2.com/products/leadtruffle/reviews)
  - The $229 entry price is a burden for very small or seasonal contractors, and the tool is text-first rather than voice — [smartaiforwork](https://www.smartaiforwork.com/tools/lead-truffle); [onelittleweb](https://onelittleweb.com/top-tools/best-roofing-answering-services/)
- **Hatch:** Opaque per-location pricing plus usage on annual contracts — [NextPhone](https://www.getnextphone.com/blog/hatch-ai-review)
- **Reddit (second-hand, via blog):**
  - An r/hvacpeople owner said most products are "a glorified voicemail that never connects to the CRM." The ones that work have real calendar integration, fast responses and emergency triage.
  - An r/HVAC thread on ServiceTitan AI dispatching doubted that most shops have a clean enough customer database. It also noted that older customers without a cell phone or email fall through text-confirmation flows — [pulpaistudio](https://pulpaistudio.com/blog/ai-voice-agent-vs-answering-service/)
- **Netic:** The $600M customer-revenue claim was made without documentation — [No Priors page](https://www.teahose.com/podcast/No%20Priors/Building%20an%20Autonomous%20Enterprise%20for%20Real-World%20Services%20with%20Netic%20Founder%20Melisa%20Tokmak)

### Inferences
- The lack of independent reviews is itself a signal. Enterprise players sell top-down to PE platforms, and SMB players are too new or small. Buyers depend on vendor case studies.
- Most ServiceTitan shops will compare any startup against ServiceTitan's native Virtual Agent, which has dispatch-board access by default.

### Gaps
- No direct Reddit threads, Facebook-group posts or YouTube reviews could be retrieved. Search results returned none, and several review sites were unreachable. Primary operator sentiment remains unverified.
- No complaint data was found for Netic, Probook or Broccoli.

## Which sub-trades and customer segments is each player not serving well?

### Takeaway
The sector skews hard toward HVAC, plumbing, electrical and roofing. Pest control, pool, septic, landscaping and garage doors are rarely named in any vendor's marketing or customer list. Segment coverage is bimodal: enterprise PE roll-ups on ServiceTitan (Avoca, Netic, Probook) vs. small shops on Jobber or Housecall Pro (Sameday, LeadTruffle, Rezora, Broccoli). The $3M–$10M independent shop that is not on ServiceTitan looks least well served by the best-funded players.

### Cited Findings
- **Avoca:** Built for mid-size to enterprise HVAC shops with a call center and ServiceTitan, and a poor fit for shops under ~$3M or with fewer than 3 CSRs. Its expansion targets restoration, auto, moving and junk removal, not pest, pool or landscaping — [Macha](https://www.getmacha.com/blog/avoca-ai-complete-guide); [gomecha](https://www.gomecha.ai/compare/avoca-ai); [Founded CEO](https://foundedceo.substack.com/p/this-yc-backed-startup-raised-52)
- **Netic:** Targets companies with $20M to billions in revenue, mainly HVAC, plumbing and roofing (plus a non-trades client, Bay Club) — [Sacra](https://sacra.com/c/netic/); [Inc.](https://www.inc.com/chloe-aiello/this-startup-gives-ai-superpowers-to-hvac-plumbing-and-roofing-companies/91266172)
- **Probook:** Every named customer is a large multi-location HVAC, plumbing or electrical platform — [GlobeNewswire](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/06/23/3316215/0/en/probook-raises-40m-from-andreessen-horowitz-and-sequoia-to-scale-the-ai-operating-system-for-home-services.html)
- **Broccoli:** Plumbing, HVAC, electrical and roofing, "and others" — [agentunfolded](https://agentunfolded.com/broccoli-52m-ai-funding/)
- **LeadTruffle:** Text-first, which is weaker for emergency voice-heavy trades. Very small or seasonal contractors find the price hard — [smartaiforwork](https://www.smartaiforwork.com/tools/lead-truffle)
- **ServiceTitan native agent:** A poor fit for shops that will never adopt the ServiceTitan FSM — [thebossmagazine summary](https://thebossmagazine.com/post/best-ai-receptionists-hvac-companies-2026/)
- **Pest control:** Generic answering-service directories cover it, but no specialist startup above names pest control customers — [onelittleweb pest control](https://onelittleweb.com/top-tools/best-pest-control-answering-services/)
- **Rilla and Siro:** Serve in-home sales (roofing, HVAC replacement, solar), not the call center. Rilla is focusing on larger operators and retail (Home Depot) — [Sacra](https://sacra.com/c/rilla)

### Inferences
- Pest control, lawn and landscaping, pool, and septic are white space among the vertical specialists. These trades tend to run on FieldRoutes, PestPac, GorillaDesk, Jobber and similar systems rather than ServiceTitan, so ServiceTitan-centric integration depth doesn't transfer. This is an inference from FSM market knowledge, not a cited source.
- Smaller Jobber and Housecall Pro shops are served mainly by low-funded tools and horizontal AI receptionists, and integration depth there is shallower than in the ServiceTitan tier.

### Gaps
- No vendor-by-vendor list of supported trades for garage doors, septic or pool was found.
- No FieldEdge or Service Fusion integration confirmations were found for any vendor.
